About Me

My research interests lie at the intersection of nonequilibrium statistical physics, soft matter, biophysics, and computational chemistry.

Research Experience

Nonequilibrium Modeling of ATP-Driven Biomolecular Systems

École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL)
Advisor: Prof. Paolo De Los Rios Jul 2026 – Present

I implement and analyze a kinetic model of the ATP-driven Hsp70 chaperone cycle to investigate how chemical energy consumption reshapes substrate binding and steady-state behavior beyond equilibrium constraints.

My work includes numerically solving the four-state reaction network under mass-conservation and thermodynamic-consistency constraints and analyzing steady-state populations, effective binding affinity, probability currents, and ultra- and infra-affinity regimes across ATP/ADP and hydrolysis-rate parameter spaces.

I am also exploring phase-field descriptions of biomolecular condensates and potential extensions connecting nonequilibrium biochemical reactions with phase-separation dynamics.

Keywords: Nonequilibrium thermodynamics · Kinetic modeling · Statistical biophysics · Hsp70 · Phase separation

Computational Design of Near-Infrared Fluorescent Proteins

University of Science and Technology of China
Advisor: Prof. Jie Zhang Jul 2025 – Mar 2026

I led a computational protein-design project aimed at designing fluorescent proteins incorporating exogenous small-molecule chromophores.

I developed an HPC-based design pipeline integrating binding-site motif construction, constrained backbone generation using RFdiffusion, sequence design using LigandMPNN, and independent structure prediction using AlphaFold.

Designed and predicted structures were compared using RMSD-based metrics and structure-based filtering criteria to prioritize candidate designs for subsequent molecular dynamics and experimental validation.

Keywords: Computational protein design · RFdiffusion · LigandMPNN · AlphaFold · HPC · Structural modeling

Molecular Dynamics Under Photoexcitation

Westlake University
Advisor: Prof. Gu Bing Jul 2025 – Aug 2025

I received training in quantum chemistry and theoretical modeling of molecular photophysical processes, with an emphasis on translating theoretical concepts into numerical calculations.

I performed electronic-structure calculations using PySCF and Python, constructed molecular models, and analyzed computed energies and potential-energy profiles for molecular systems.

Keywords: Quantum chemistry · Electronic structure · PySCF · Python · Molecular modeling
